Besties Art Date

Calabash painting & conversations with inspiring women in celebration of the International Day of the Girl Child.

On the 25th of October 2026, our Besties gathered at Glaze Art Studio for a fun yet much-needed experience that brought creativity and mentorship together.

The Besties Art Date was organised to bridge the gap between the futures our girls envision for themselves and the lived experiences of women who have pursued their ambitions. This vision was inspired by our “Dear Future Me” series, created in celebration of the International Day of the Girl Child. Through this initiative, we hoped to encourage our girls to dream boldly while understanding the effort it takes to turn those dreams into reality.

Our mentors

For the Besties Art Date, we were joined by Aggor Yorm, Founder of Besties, TV Director and Producer; Adwoa Noella, Media Personality; and Kezia Sanie Kermah, Esq., Founder and President of For the Future Organization. These three inspiring women served as mentors to our Besties.

Through enlightening conversations, our mentors shared their personal journeys, experiences, and the steps that led them to where they are today. Our Besties also shared their aspirations, talked about what they hope to become, and asked questions about the journeys ahead of them. It was a beautiful opportunity to learn, connect, and explore the possibilities that lie ahead.

Painting, questions & laughter

Beyond the conversations, the girls got creative with calabash painting, guided by their instructors. Between the painting, questions, and laughter, the afternoon was a reminder that mentorship doesn't always have to happen in a formal setting. Sometimes, all it takes is a shared activity, an open conversation, and the opportunity to learn from someone else’s story.

It was an intimate and enjoyable experience that created space for the girls to express themselves, ask questions, and begin connecting the futures they imagine with the realities of pursuing their ambitions.
