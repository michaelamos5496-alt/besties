Besties on Marriage is on YouTube

Our Besties talk about child marriage and their dreams for the future, for International Day of the Girl Child.

What will you say if Mummy told you, “Tomorrow morning, 9:00 AM, we are going to your wedding”?

This was one of the thought-provoking questions our Besties had to sit down and answer to gain insight into how girls around the world are faced with that same reality, without having a choice or a say in the matter.

As we joined girls around the world to observe the International Day of the Girl Child on October 11, 2026, our conversations focused on the global theme “End Child Marriage and Invest in Girls' Rights.”

Each Bestie was presented with questions and scenarios that encouraged her to think about what it would mean to have the freedom to choose her own future or have that freedom taken away. While our girls had the opportunity to express their opinions, many girls around the world are denied that very choice.

When asked how they felt about girls in this situation, some responded by saying, “the girls will suffer because they are not knowledgeable about marriage”. Some expressed surprise, and most felt sad.

When also asked what age they thought was appropriate for marriage, our Besties individually agreed that marriage should happen in their twenties or thirties. They believe that at this stage in life, girls are mature enough to make such an important decision for themselves.

However, we didn’t just end there.

In our new series titled “Dear Future Me,” our Besties wrote about the futures they envision for themselves. They shared their dreams, career aspirations, where they see themselves in 20 years, and even the beautiful homes, cars, and infinity pools they hope to own someday.

Through these conversations, we were reminded that even at their young age, girls have dreams too, which they hope to achieve someday, and rights that should not be denied.

Our Besties also had a message for parents who force their daughters into early marriages:

“Let them go to school so their future will be bright.”

“Please let this stop from now onwards.”

“Let them spend time with their best friends.”

Watch the full conversation on our YouTube channel.

Happy International Day of the Girl Child, Besties!
