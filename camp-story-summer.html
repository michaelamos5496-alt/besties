Summer Besties Camp: A Week of Firsts

60 girls spent six days hiking Aburi's trails, sleeping under the stars, and cheering each other through a ropes course most had never attempted. By the closing campfire, cabins that started as strangers were trading contact info and planning reunions.

Mornings started with trail hikes and trust-building games, while afternoons were reserved for the ropes course — the one activity nobody expected to love as much as they did. Evenings wound down around the campfire with songs, s'mores, and cabin chats that ran long past lights-out. By the final morning, camper journals were full of new nicknames, inside jokes, and promises to meet again next summer.
