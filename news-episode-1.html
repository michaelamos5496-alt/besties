Besties Camp Episode 1 is on YouTube

The first episode of the Besties Camp reality experience is out now.

The Besties Camp reality experience has officially begun. Episode 1, titled "First Day of Besties Camp is Here", is now live on YouTube.

When the first Besties Summer Camp took place, the cameras were rolling for the entire experience. Twelve girls, including the four original Besties from the Best Girlfriends podcast, spent three days and two nights together, and the whole thing was captured by Egli Studios as a reality experience.

What to expect

Episode 1 takes you back to where it all started: the first day of camp, as the girls got their first taste of life together. Along the way, the girls shared their thoughts and reactions in diary-room sessions as they worked through friendships, challenges, competition and life under one roof.

More episodes on the way

This is only the beginning. Further episodes are on the way, and you can find them all on the Besties Summer Camp story page as they are released.
