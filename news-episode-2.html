Besties Camp Episode 2 is on YouTube

The second episode of the Besties Camp reality experience is out now.

The Besties Camp reality experience continues. Episode 2, titled "The Girls Get Real About Growing Up", is now live on YouTube, following Episode 1.

Growing up, out loud

Growing up was an important part of the first Besties Summer Camp. Alongside the games, challenges and creative sessions, the girls joined a special conversation about menstrual hygiene and growing up with a former Miss Malaika Ghana queen, a chance to ask questions and learn in a safe, supportive space.

Watch the series

The whole camp was captured by Egli Studios as a reality experience, with the girls sharing their thoughts and reactions in diary-room sessions along the way. You can find the episodes released so far on the Besties Summer Camp story page, and more are on the way.
