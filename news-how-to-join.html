How to join Besties: a step-by-step guide

Everything parents and girls need to know about applying, what membership includes and what it costs.

Thinking about joining the Besties community? Here is how it works, from the first form to the welcome pack.

Who can join

Right now, Besties experiences are created for girls aged 8 to 12. The community is growing, and more age groups will be welcomed as it does. Every application needs a parent or guardian who is aware of and supports it.

How to apply

  1. Fill out the application. Share your name, phone number, age and email.
  2. Submit a short video. Send a short video about yourself, on the website or on WhatsApp.
  3. Wait to be selected. If you are chosen, we will reach out to you directly.
  4. Finish up on WhatsApp. The rest of the process, including payment and next steps, continues on WhatsApp.

What membership includes

The Besties annual membership is GHS 500 a year and includes:

  • A Besties Club welcome pack, including a Besties T-shirt, Lacoste and headband
  • Access to members-only activities and experiences
  • Priority access and exclusive discounts on Besties events and merchandise
  • Opportunities to be featured in Besties audio visual contents
  • Being part of a growing community of girls learning, growing and shining together

What about other costs?

Some events, workshops, experiences and merchandise need separate registration or purchase. We will always share the cost and all the details with you before you pay.
