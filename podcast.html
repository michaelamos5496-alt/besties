The Best Gurlies Podcast

The Best Gurlies podcast is honest conversations by 10-year-olds: Kelly, Viphine, Babette and Elaine.

The podcast brings girls into a world of laughter, relatable conversations, fun challenges, and real talk about friendship, confidence, school, family, and everything in between. Through their unique personalities and shared experiences, the girls inspire their peers to express themselves, celebrate their individuality, and discover the joy of girlhood.
