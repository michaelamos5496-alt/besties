What Besties Camp Is All About

Besties Camp is an immersive residential experience where girls come together to make new friends, discover new interests, learn new skills and create unforgettable memories.

Each camp brings a new theme, adventure and experience, with every edition thoughtfully designed to blend friendship, creativity, learning, confidence and lots of fun in an environment where girls can be themselves, try new things and build meaningful connections within the Besties community.

🏕️

Outdoor Adventure

Hiking, campfires, team games, and hands-on outdoor skills in a safe, supervised environment.

💪

Confidence Building

Every activity is designed to help campers try new things and discover what they're capable of.

🤝

Lifelong Friendships

Cabin bonding, group challenges, and shared campfire nights turn strangers into Besties for life.

🛡️

Safe & Supervised

Trained, background-checked staff and small group ratios keep every camper safe and cared for.
