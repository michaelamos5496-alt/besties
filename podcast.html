The Story Behind the Podcast

It started with a phone call. While speaking with Ola, mum to one of the original Besties, our founder Aggor Yorm overheard a voice note playing in the background. It was Kelly and her friends, nine years old, chatting away in a WhatsApp group.

They were funny, expressive, curious and completely themselves. "There is a show here," she thought.

That voice note became Best Girlfriends, a podcast with four girls, created to give young girls a space to talk, share, learn and be heard. The show moved from YouTube to traditional television, and the end of the first season was celebrated with a live party.

Along the way it became clear we were building something bigger than a podcast. The girls didn't just want to watch, they wanted to be part of it. That is how the Besties community was born.
