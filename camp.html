What Besties Camp Is All About

Besties Camp is where girls disconnect from screens and reconnect with themselves — trying new things, making mistakes safely, and finding out just how capable they are. Every session blends outdoor adventure with the same confidence-building spirit at the heart of everything Besties does, all under the care of trained, background-checked staff.

🏕️

Outdoor Adventure

Hiking, campfires, team games, and hands-on outdoor skills in a safe, supervised environment.

💪

Confidence Building

Every activity is designed to help campers try new things and discover what they're capable of.

🤝

Lifelong Friendships

Cabin bonding, group challenges, and shared campfire nights turn strangers into Besties for life.

🛡️

Safe & Supervised

Trained, background-checked staff and small group ratios keep every camper safe and cared for.

Summer Besties Camp: A Week of Firsts

60 girls spent six days hiking Aburi's trails, sleeping under the stars, and cheering each other through a ropes course most had never attempted. By the closing campfire, cabins that started as strangers were trading contact info and planning reunions.

Easter Camp Getaway Recap

A long-weekend camp packed with egg-hunt challenges, team relays, and a talent night that ran an hour past schedule because nobody wanted it to end. Campers left with new best friends and a few new songs stuck in their heads.
