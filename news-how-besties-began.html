From a WhatsApp voice note to a community for girls

How a phone call about a group chat turned into Besties.

Every now and then, an idea arrives in the most unexpected way. For Besties, it began with a phone call.

A voice note that clicked

Aggor Yorm, founder of Egli Studios and Besties, was on the phone with Ola, the mother of Kelly, one of the girls who would become an original Bestie. In the background, Yorm heard a voice note playing and asked who was speaking. It was Kelly and her friends chatting in a WhatsApp group.

These nine-year-old girls were funny, expressive, curious and completely themselves. Yorm remembers thinking there was a show there.

From podcast to television

That conversation became Best Girlfriends, a podcast featuring four girls, created to give young girls a space to talk, share, learn and be heard. The show grew from YouTube to traditional television, and the end of the first season was celebrated with a live party.

The girls wanted in

As the team prepared for Season 2, it became clear that the girls did not just want to watch Besties. They wanted to be part of it. That is how the Besties community was born: a growing community for girls, currently creating experiences for girls aged 8 to 12.

Camps, events, content and mentorship

Today, Besties creates camps, events, content, mentorship and carefully curated experiences that help girls grow in confidence, communication, creativity, independence, collaboration and self-expression. The first Besties Summer Camp, where twelve girls lived, learned, created and competed together for 60 hours, was the first big step in bringing that vision to life.

The women behind Besties

Behind Besties is a carefully selected team of women who share the vision: producers, teachers, creatives, chaperones and mentors who are building meaningful things of their own.
