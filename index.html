Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you may want to know about becoming a Bestie.

What is the Besties Club?

Besties is a growing community where girls build confidence, make real friendships and discover who they are through creativity, learning and meaningful experiences.

How can my child join?

Here's how it works:

  1. Fill out the application form with your name, phone number, age and email.
  2. Submit a short video about yourself, on the website or on WhatsApp.
  3. Wait to be selected — if chosen, we'll reach out to you directly and welcome you into the community for free.
  4. Finish up on WhatsApp, where we'll confirm you're in and keep you posted on upcoming camps and events.

Requirements: girls aged 8–12, with a parent or guardian aware of the application, a short video introduction, and access to WhatsApp for updates.

Do I have to pay money?

Joining the Besties community is free right now — we're focused on building that community first through camps and events. Individual experiences like camps and events have their own fee, always shared upfront. Besties Annual Membership is coming soon.

What do we do?

We create camps, events, content, mentorship and carefully curated experiences that help girls grow in confidence, communication, creativity, independence, collaboration and self-expression.

What do I get by joining?

You become part of a supportive community and get first access to camps and events, plus opportunities to make friends, try new things, learn new skills and create memories with other Besties. Annual membership, with its own welcome pack and perks, is coming soon.

If I am 7 or 16, can I join?

Right now, our experiences are created for girls aged 8–12. We are growing with our Besties and plan to welcome more age groups as the community grows.

Have Questions? Let's Talk

Whether it's about camps, events, partnerships, or just saying hi — we'd love to hear from you. Reach out and let's connect.
