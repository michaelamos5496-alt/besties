Our First Camp

Besties Summer Camp: 60 Hours of Friendship, Fun & Unforgettable Moments

The very first Besties Summer Camp is officially in the books! 💕✨

For three days and two nights, 12 girls, including the four original Besties from the Best Girlfriends podcast, came together for 60 hours of friendship, creativity, learning, challenges and plenty of fun.

From a lively welcome party with a female DJ to pottery and beading sessions, cooking challenges, fitness and outdoor games, the girls had a packed experience designed to let them learn, create, connect and simply enjoy being girls.

There were also meaningful moments along the way, including a special conversation on menstrual hygiene and growing up with a former Miss Malaika Ghana queen, as well as a beading masterclass with designer Kofi Akotoa.

And because this is Besties, the cameras were rolling! 🎥 The entire camp was captured as a reality experience, with the girls sharing their thoughts and reactions through diary-room sessions as they navigated friendships, challenges, competition and life under one roof. The Besties Camp reality experience will be coming to screens soon.

For Aggor Yorm, Camp Director, Besties Summer Camp, the experience was an opportunity to bring the community's vision to life beyond the podcast:

"Besties Camp was created to bring our vision of community to life beyond the podcast. We wanted the girls to have a space where they could make new friends, try new things, learn, create and simply enjoy being girls. Seeing that come to life over those 60 hours was incredibly special, and it's only the beginning of what we hope to build with Besties."

— Aggor Yorm, Camp Director, Besties Summer Camp

Most importantly, the camp brought the vision of Besties as a community to life: a space where girls can make new friends, discover themselves, try new things and create memories together.

One camp. 12 girls. 60 hours. So many memories.

And this is only the beginning.
