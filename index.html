Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you may want to know about becoming a Bestie.

What is the Besties Club?

Besties is a growing community where girls build confidence, make real friendships and discover who they are through creativity, learning and meaningful experiences.

How can my child join?

Here's how it works:

  1. Fill out the application form with your name, phone number, age and email.
  2. Submit a short video about yourself, on the website or on WhatsApp.
  3. Wait to be selected — if chosen, we'll reach out to you directly. A program fee applies for selected Besties.
  4. Finish up on WhatsApp, where the rest of the process, including payment and next steps, continues.

Requirements: girls aged 8–12, with a parent or guardian aware of the application, a short video introduction, and access to WhatsApp for updates.

Do I have to pay money?

To be a part of the Besties Club itself, you'll pay an annual fee, and then there are separate fees for individual experiences like camps and events. We will always share the cost and all the details with you before you pay.

What do we do?

We create camps, events, content, mentorship and carefully curated experiences that help girls grow in confidence, communication, creativity, independence, collaboration and self-expression.

What do I get as a member?

Members receive a Besties Club welcome pack, including a Besties T-shirt, Lacoste and headband. You also become part of a supportive community and get opportunities to make friends, try new things, learn new skills and create memories with other Besties.

If I am 7 or 16, can I join?

Right now, our experiences are created for girls aged 8–12. We are growing with our Besties and plan to welcome more age groups as the community grows.
